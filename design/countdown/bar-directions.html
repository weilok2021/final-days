Countdown: where the life bar goes now

The always-on bar leaves web pages. The countdown is the one place it still appears, so it should be part of the message rather than a hairline at the edge. Each mockup is the countdown as a Chrome tab at 16:9; the sizes are the real overlay's (the big number is 16 % of the viewport height). Put in a date of birth and every bar updates. Colours are the spec's: green through yellow to red for the days lived, #27272a for the days left.

Recommendation: A

The bar left the pages because a line at an edge stops registering. Putting it back at an edge, only thicker, repeats that mistake inside the countdown. Under the number it is looked at every time the number is, and the existing line already says what it measures, so no labels are needed. D is the runner-up if you want the day count to sit on the bar itself.
