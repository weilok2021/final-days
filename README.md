# Final Days

**Every day left is one of your final days.**

You get about 30,000 days. Final Days puts that number in front of you: a
4-pixel bar along the top edge of your screen that fills from green to red as
your life passes, and once a day, at the first unlock, a full-screen moment
showing how many days you have left.

No installer. One file. Nothing outside its own folder.

## Status

Version 1, Windows first. In progress.

The behaviour is defined once in a spec so that ports (macOS, a browser
extension) can follow it without touching the Windows code.
